\section{Conclusion}

Knoema Engine demonstrates a compact open runtime for persistent social agents. The Phase 30 version expands the original technical report with scenario DSL artifacts, SDK surfaces, a formal benchmark bundle, a 50-agent deterministic experiment, documentation infrastructure, and a stricter safety boundary. The next research step is to replace deterministic proxy evaluation with controlled provider-backed runs, stronger semantic retrieval, human review of plausibility and safety, and fairer external baseline reproduction.
