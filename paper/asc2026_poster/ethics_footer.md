# Ethics Footer (poster + abstract)

The footer below is the canonical text to attach to the ASC poster, the
abstract submission portal notes field, and any derivative slide deck.

---

This work uses fictional, synthetic, non-identifying scenarios only. It is
not a crime prediction system and does not produce per-person risk scores.
The Luvoire engine on which this study is built declines defense, predictive-
policing, PSYOP, non-consenting real-person simulation, and minors-in-
synthetic-pipelines use categories by published policy
(POLICIES/civilian_use.md, 2026-04-23). The replay-equality table on the
poster is single-sourced from scripts/verify_replay_shas.py; the sensitivity
panel is single-sourced from experiments/rat_sensitivity/results/sobol_indices.json.
