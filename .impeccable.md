## Design Context

### Users
Luvoire's public site serves research evaluators, professors, simulation researchers, game developers, and open-source contributors. They arrive to judge credibility, inspect reproducible artifacts, try demos, and understand how the same engine applies to games, fictional public-safety replay research, and academic simulation.

### Brand Personality
Precise, civic, and builder-led. The interface should feel evidence-first and technically serious without becoming cold, defensive, or hype-driven.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use an editorial technical-atlas direction: light, readable, structured, and image-led, with measured green, coral, and teal accents. Avoid generic dark neon, purple-blue gradients, decorative blobs, beige palettes, and template SaaS hero layouts.

### Design Principles
- Start with the actual project surface: demos, reports, code, and integration paths.
- Make the three application tracks obvious without overstating production readiness.
- Prefer proof artifacts over marketing claims.
- Keep safety language visible for synthetic public-safety examples.
- Maintain responsive, accessible layouts with stable components and clear focus states.
