# CAT-28 Bibliographic Sources

Composite reference list covering every `literature_sources` entry in the personality archetype yamls under `single/` and `composite/`. Each entry here is independently citable via DOI, ISBN, or ISSN.

## Monographs

- Azuma, Hiroki (2009). *Otaku: Japan's Database Animals* (translated by Jonathan E. Abel and Shion Kono). Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press. ISBN 978-0-8166-5352-2.
- Condry, Ian (2013). *The Soul of Anime: Collaborative Creativity and Japan's Media Success Story*. Durham: Duke University Press. ISBN 978-0-8223-5380-3.
- Galbraith, Patrick W. (2014). *The Moe Manifesto: An Insider's Look at the Worlds of Manga, Anime, and Gaming*. Tokyo: Tuttle Publishing. ISBN 978-4-8053-1282-8.
- Galbraith, Patrick W. (2019). *Otaku and the Struggle for Imagination in Japan*. Durham: Duke University Press. ISBN 978-1-4780-0560-7.
- Saito, Tamaki (2011). *Beautiful Fighting Girl* (translated by J. Keith Vincent and Dawn Lawson from the 2000 Japanese original). Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press. ISBN 978-0-8166-6459-7.

## Exclusions

- True-crime or forensic-profiling literature is excluded from CAT-28 and must be kept in the criminology namespaces (`tier1_archetypes/`, `tier2_historical/`).
- Pop-culture blog posts, fan-run wikis, and similar non-peer sources are excluded.
- No AI-generated summaries were used to derive any archetype description in this directory.
