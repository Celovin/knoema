# Archetype Profile Ethics

## 1. Tier policy

Only Tier 1 and Tier 2 profiles may be generated in this repository.

- Tier 1, archetype: behavioral profile derived from published academic typologies, with no link to any real individual.
- Tier 2, historical canonical: named historical figures whose cases are canonical in English-language criminology literature, pre-1930, with no living direct victims and no immediate descendant impact. The only Codex-generated Tier 2 profiles allowed here are Whitechapel 1888, H.H. Holmes, and Belle Gunness.
- Tier 3, 20th-century named academic case: reserved for manual user addition only. Codex must not create these profiles.
- Tier 4, contemporary or Korean cases: permanently forbidden in this repository. Codex must reject creation and stop with a blocker entry.

## 2. Ethical disclaimer

This profile is a pedagogical, synthetic replay overlay for criminology education. It is not an operational profiling tool, a prediction, or guidance for identifying any real person.

## 3. Legal and product framing

Profiles are for source criticism, routine-activity teaching, and offline demonstration. They must not be used for suspect identification, public accusation, employment screening, insurance decisions, policing triage, or real-world risk scoring. Korean Civil Act Articles 750 and 751 are relevant defamation and tort-risk reminders: a public system that suggests a real person's criminality can create legal exposure even when framed as analysis.

## 4. Operational limits

Profiles must be additive overlays that never alter the committed base replay. They must not include graphic conduct, victim names, living-person names, operational instructions, scripts, payment routes, evasion details, or contemporary incident analogies.
