# Design Review Focus

## Purpose

Fictional design review scenario for separating taste from requirements.

## Setup

- Scenario ID: `workplace_design_review_focus`
- Domain: `academic_research`
- Category: `workplace`
- Agents: `agent_a` and `agent_b`, both synthetic fictional participants

## Event Focus

`work.design_checkpoint` records this checkpoint: Reviewers tag comments as requirement, risk, or preference.

## Metric

`review_precision` is a scenario-specific score for inspecting replay quality and interaction clarity.

## Safety

Ethics guardrail complete: fictional agents only, no real people, no forecasting, no scoring of suspects.
