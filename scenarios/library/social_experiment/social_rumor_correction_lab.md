# Rumor Correction Lab

## Purpose

Fictional communication scenario for correcting uncertain claims carefully.

## Setup

- Scenario ID: `social_rumor_correction_lab`
- Domain: `academic_research`
- Category: `social_experiment`
- Agents: `agent_a` and `agent_b`, both synthetic fictional participants

## Event Focus

`social.rumor_checkpoint` records this checkpoint: A participant marks a claim as unverified and suggests a source check.

## Metric

`correction_care` is a scenario-specific score for inspecting replay quality and interaction clarity.

## Safety

Ethics guardrail complete: fictional agents only, no real people, no forecasting, no scoring of suspects. This social-experiment scenario is for education or research design review only and requires review before any human study.
