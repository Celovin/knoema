# Apartment Notice Review

## Purpose

Fictional apartment community scenario for reviewing a shared notice.

## Setup

- Scenario ID: `community_apartment_notice`
- Domain: `academic_research`
- Category: `community`
- Agents: `agent_a` and `agent_b`, both synthetic fictional participants

## Event Focus

`community.notice_checkpoint` records this checkpoint: Residents rewrite a notice to make it clearer and calmer.

## Metric

`notice_clarity` is a scenario-specific score for inspecting replay quality and interaction clarity.

## Safety

Ethics guardrail complete: fictional agents only, no real people, no forecasting, no scoring of suspects.
