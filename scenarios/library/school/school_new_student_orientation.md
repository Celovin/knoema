# New Student Orientation

## Purpose

Fictional orientation replay for welcoming a new student into a homeroom.

## Setup

- Scenario ID: `school_new_student_orientation`
- Domain: `academic_research`
- Category: `school`
- Agents: `agent_a` and `agent_b`, both synthetic fictional participants

## Event Focus

`orientation.welcome_checkpoint` records this checkpoint: A class guide explains the schedule and checks whether the newcomer needs help.

## Metric

`welcome_coverage` is a scenario-specific score for inspecting replay quality and interaction clarity.

## Safety

Ethics guardrail complete: fictional agents only, no real people, no forecasting, no scoring of suspects.
