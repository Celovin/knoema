# Safety Scenario Guide

Luvoire can model fictional replay scenarios, but the public boundary is strict.

## Allowed

- Fictional agents.
- Synthetic locations.
- Non-identifying events.
- Prevention-oriented replay analysis.
- Reproducibility, auditability, and education demos.

## Not Allowed

- Real incident records.
- Victim, suspect, witness, address, school, hospital, or family details.
- Prediction of future crime.
- Suspect scoring or profiling.
- Surveillance or enforcement automation.
- Claims that a simulation reveals real-world guilt, intent, or risk.

## Scenario Metadata

Every sensitive scenario should document:

- Synthetic data source.
- Intended research or education purpose.
- Explicit non-use boundary.
- Review notes for privacy and harm reduction.

## Review Checklist

1. All entities are fictional or fully consented.
2. No real identifying details are present.
3. The output cannot be used to rank or score real people.
4. The scenario has a clear prevention or education purpose.
5. Logs and exports are safe to publish.
