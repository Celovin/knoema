# Theory of Mind Development

## Research Question

Do perspective-taking traits and planning depth improve false-belief-consistent behavior in deterministic Knoema social simulations?

## Hypotheses

- H1. Agents with higher perspective-taking and humanism scores will resolve false-belief conflicts more accurately.
- H2. Higher planning depth will increase belief-state consistency across repeated seeds.

## IV/DV

- IV: perspective-taking profile, planning depth, false-belief event condition.
- DV: false-belief-consistent action rate, belief repair latency, explanation consistency.

## Sample Size

Use at least 128 seed-condition observations. Run the Playground power analysis with a two-proportion test, effect size 0.35, alpha 0.05, and target power 0.80.

## Analysis Plan

Use paired seed comparisons for planning depth and independent comparisons for trait profiles. Report effect sizes, confidence intervals, and corrected p-values.

## Submission Checklist

- Scenario YAML, seed schedule, and false-belief event injections are frozen.
- All exclusion rules are documented before running the batch.
- JSONL digest and reproducibility certificate are attached.
