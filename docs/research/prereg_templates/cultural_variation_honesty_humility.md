# Cultural Variation in Honesty-Humility

## Research Question

How do cultural-prior shifts in Honesty-Humility change cooperation and deception-related action rates?

## Hypotheses

- H1. Cultural-prior shifts in Honesty-Humility will change cooperation and deception-related action rates.
- H2. Effects will remain directionally stable after controlling for agent count and scenario type.

## IV/DV

- IV: cultural-prior condition, scenario family, matched seed.
- DV: cooperation rate, deception-like actions, trust delta, conflict repair.

## Sample Size

Use at least 160 matched seed-condition observations. Run the Playground power analysis with a paired t-test, effect size 0.40, alpha 0.05, and target power 0.80.

## Analysis Plan

Use matched-seed comparisons and report effect sizes with caveats that cultural priors are synthetic modeling assumptions.

## Submission Checklist

- Cultural-prior IDs and seed ranges are frozen.
- Scenario families are balanced across conditions.
- Cultural interpretation caveats are placed before claims.
