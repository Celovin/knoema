# Knoema Refund and Cancellation Policy v1

> STATUS: DRAFT - LEGAL REVIEW PENDING (do not publish)

This policy is a draft for Knoema commercial subscriptions and metered services. It must be reviewed against Korean e-commerce law, unfair terms rules, payment processor contracts, and B2B order form mechanics before publication.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to paid Knoema subscriptions, metered usage, and enterprise contracts unless an order form or separate signed agreement states otherwise. It does not apply to open-source code, free demos, Customer's own LLM provider charges, or third-party provider fees.

## 2. Unused Refund Within 7 Days

Customer may request a full refund within 7 days after payment if Customer has not used paid features, metered LLM calls, team invitations, enterprise deployment, or downloadable paid deliverables.

After paid use begins, Celovin does not provide prorated refunds by default. Exceptions may apply where Celovin materially fails to provide the purchased service, applicable law requires a refund, or an order form states a different rule.

## 3. Monthly Subscription Cancellation

Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled before the next billing date. Customer may continue using the Service until the end of the paid monthly term. Celovin does not provide prorated refunds for a monthly term that has already started.

## 4. Annual Subscription Refunds

Annual subscriptions may be refunded only within 30 days after payment. The refund amount may be reduced for months used, metered usage, discounts, fees, and tax effects. After 30 days, Celovin does not provide a prorated refund for remaining months unless required by law or a signed order form.

## 5. Payment Errors and Duplicate Charges

If Celovin confirms a duplicate charge, authorization error, or payment processor error, Celovin will process cancellation or refund through the relevant provider, such as Toss Payments, Paddle, or Stripe.

## 6. Refund Flow

1. Customer submits the account email, payment receipt, order ID, and refund reason through the support channel.
2. Celovin reviews usage, payment date, subscription type, metered consumption, and legal obligations.
3. Korean domestic payments are processed through Toss Payments cancellation or refund flow.
4. Global payments are processed through Paddle seller dashboard or API flow.
5. Actual settlement timing depends on the card issuer, bank, and payment processor.

## 7. Statutory Rights

This policy does not limit mandatory rights under applicable law, including Article 7 of Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce. B2B enterprise contracts may be governed by the applicable order form and signed agreement.
