# University Course Kit

Course title: LLM-Based Multi-Agent Social Simulation

This 12-week kit is designed for upper-level undergraduate or graduate courses in AI systems, computational social science, game AI, HCI, or research methods. Students use Knoema to design scenarios, run reproducible simulations, audit behavior, and present a final study.

## Course Outcomes

- Build and validate Scenario DSL YAML.
- Explain how personality traits, memory, relationships, and events shape simulated actions.
- Run deterministic experiments and attach reproducibility certificates.
- Evaluate trait-action correlations, fairness audit outputs, and theory-of-mind probes.
- Package a final project with code, report, presentation, and replication artifacts.

## Week 1 - Scenario Authoring and Local Runs

Students install Knoema, run a two-agent scenario, and edit a safe Scenario DSL file.

Assignment: [Week 01 Scenario Authoring](assignments/week_01_scenario_authoring.md)

## Week 2 - Persona and Trait Design

Students compare Big Five, HEXACO, moral foundations, and Schwartz values in a small cast.

Assignment: [Week 02 Persona Trait Design](assignments/week_02_persona_trait_design.md)

## Week 3 - Trait Slider Experiment

Students isolate one trait manipulation and measure the resulting action distribution.

Assignment: [Week 03 Trait Slider Experiment](assignments/week_03_trait_slider_experiment.md)

## Week 4 - Events, Routines, and Environment Context

Students add scheduled events, location changes, and routine conflicts.

Assignment: [Week 04 Events and Routines](assignments/week_04_events_and_routines.md)

## Week 5 - Hierarchical Planning Analysis

Students enable HTN planning and inspect plan decomposition against observed actions.

Assignment: [Week 05 HTN Planning Analysis](assignments/week_05_htn_planning_analysis.md)

## Week 6 - Relationship Graphs and Conversation Threads

Students analyze relationship edge changes and directed conversation sequences.

Assignment: [Week 06 Relationship Analysis](assignments/week_06_relationship_analysis.md)

## Week 7 - Memory Retrieval Benchmark

Students design a memory retrieval probe and compare short-term and long-term evidence.

Assignment: [Week 07 Memory Retrieval Benchmark](assignments/week_07_memory_retrieval_benchmark.md)

## Week 8 - Batch Runs and Statistical Summaries

Students run multiple seeds, export CSV/LaTeX tables, and summarize uncertainty.

Assignment: [Week 08 Batch Statistics](assignments/week_08_batch_statistics.md)

## Week 9 - Theory of Mind Replication

Students reproduce a Sally-Anne-style false-belief probe with synthetic agents.

Assignment: [Week 09 Theory of Mind Replication](assignments/week_09_theory_of_mind_replication.md)

## Week 10 - Fairness and Safety Audit

Students use trait-action heatmaps, content guardrails, and audit logs to critique risks.

Assignment: [Week 10 Fairness Safety Audit](assignments/week_10_fairness_safety_audit.md)

## Week 11 - Final Project Proposal

Students freeze a final question, preregistration draft, and replication plan.

Assignment: [Week 11 Final Project Proposal](assignments/week_11_final_project_proposal.md)

## Week 12 - Final Project Presentation

Students present a replication-ready final project with artifacts and limitations.

Assignment: [Week 12 Final Project Presentation](assignments/week_12_final_project_presentation.md)
