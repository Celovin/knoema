# Week 02 - Persona Trait Design

## Learning Goal

Explain how personality dimensions become testable simulation inputs.

## Task

Design three agents with distinct Big Five, HEXACO, moral-foundation, and Schwartz-value profiles.

## Deliverables

- Trait table for every agent.
- Scenario YAML or Playground export.
- Short rationale linking traits to hypothesized actions.

## Grading

- 30% complete trait vectors.
- 40% defensible trait rationale.
- 20% safe fictional framing.
- 10% artifact organization.
