# Week 09 - Theory of Mind Replication

## Learning Goal

Reproduce a false-belief-style theory-of-mind probe with synthetic agents.

## Task

Adapt a Sally-Anne-style scenario and record whether agent actions reflect the hidden belief state.

## Deliverables

- Scenario config.
- Expected belief-state table.
- JSONL evidence.
- 900-word replication note.

## Grading

- 30% probe design.
- 40% evidence quality.
- 20% discussion of alternative explanations.
- 10% reproducibility notes.
